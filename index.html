FAQs

Questions students ask

Quick answers to common questions. For anything else, message us on WhatsApp.

What does it cost to join?

Fees depend on the course and batch. Our admissions team will share the current fee details and what the one-time registration fee covers when you apply. Message us on WhatsApp at 0329-0739313.

How long is a course?

12 weeks: 8 weeks of live online training, then 4 weeks for the final project and assessment.

Who can apply?

Anyone in Pakistan with Matric or higher education. No IT background is needed.

Are the classes online?

Yes. All courses run 100% online. Classes are live and every lecture is saved so you can revise any time.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Pass the final project and assessment and you receive a certificate with a code that anyone can verify.

Is the registration fee refundable?

Refund conditions are explained in our Terms & Conditions. Please read them before you apply.
